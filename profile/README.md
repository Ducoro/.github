# Ducoro

**Group your Bots in Ducoro.**

Keep your things moving and get your attention back.

[Website](https://ducoro.ai)

[中文介绍](https://github.com/Ducoro/.github/blob/main/profile/README.zh-CN.md)

Ducoro brings your Bots together around the work you entrust to them. A Lead coordinates the work, brings back results, and comes to you when a decision needs your judgment.

- **Hand over the goal.** Explain what you want to achieve and the boundaries that matter.
- **Keep the work moving.** Let your Bots handle ongoing execution and coordination.
- **Keep the final say.** Review results and make the decisions that belong to you.

## Open source

[**Official plugins**](https://github.com/Ducoro/plugins) extend what your Bots can do. The catalog is maintained here on GitHub and updated independently of the Ducoro app.

Explore the plugins, read their instructions, and contribute through the repository's contribution guide.
